## 关键互证与反差

1. **自报置信≠概率（三文两证+文献族）**：MafiaScope ECE=0.168（自报 80-99 实际 54.6%）；Confident 高置信吃子 1/62 正确（Brier 比 uniform 7.1×）+ConfidenceBench 15 模型 5 个劣于 calibrated-random；共同机制假说=RLHF 高置信偏置（Confident 引 [9]）/不忠实自报（MafiaScope 引 Turpin 2023）→我们「LLM 出逻辑不出数字+自报置信不直接采信+结算后校准」纪律获 2026-07/08 三重独立佐证。
2. **信念可用但不被跟随**：Auditing consistency 0.207 且强制跟随反而降 WR；Confident 73.6% 吃子目标是自报分布从未列过的格（coverage gap）；MafiaScope F4 探针-投票耦合 64.9% 但 Mafia 无增益——三条互证：**信念信号与行动解耦是普遍现象**，我们的 deviation 记账（Auditing 三分类可借）+置信带分层校准（Confident 99.3% 集中性）都有直接落点。
3. **测量扰动自警**：MafiaScope 重问冻结 flip 34.8%（已挂账 L1：同法测我们三路判词采样随机占比）；Confident 引同一数字作 split-elicitation 噪声证据——joint vs split 引出消融须进我们实验序列。
4. **常规指标与信念能力解耦**：Confident 4.4（全胜配置=最差信念质量，3.8% belief rate）+Auditing 6.6（负载混淆造虚假战略结论）→我们烟测/工程指标不能替代结算管线，且必须记录采样配置与并发。

## Related work 段落草案（直接可用底稿，标【引申】处为本棒起草话术）

> 【已核事实底座】Recent work measures LLM beliefs in hidden-information games along three distinct lines. Probing instruments elicit beliefs non-invasively and score them against engine ground truth: MafiaScope [2607.10645] attaches a YAML-configured probe battery after every public utterance in Mafia, never re-entering gameplay, and finds stated confidence poorly calibrated (ECE=0.168; accuracy 54.6% where confidence claims 80-99) and second-order judgments overestimated 1.53×. Auditability frameworks maintain an external belief layer alongside the agent: [2607.10814] updates factorized log-odds beliefs from structured events in a 9-player Werewolf with code-level information isolation, shows an active-belief condition associated with higher good-side win rate (0.205→0.390, McNemar p<0.001) while direct action-belief consistency stays at 0.207, and attributes nothing to belief content. Settlement instruments score elicited probability distributions against recoverable truth at the moment of action: [2608.24691] elicits, every turn, a distribution over a secretly relocated hidden state in a chess variant and finds captures made at ≥0.5 stated confidence correct in 1 of 62 cases across two pre-registered batches (Brier 7.1× a population-matched uniform prior, 99.3% of the gap concentrated in high-confidence events), under an oracle-vs-public tractability bound that separates model failure from task impossibility.

> 【引申·我们填的缝】These lines share two structural gaps this work addresses. First, none closes the settlement loop: elicited probabilities are diagnosed (ECE, Brier) but never recalibrated and re-scored, no work aggregates multiple belief trials in logit space with prior shrinkage, and none reports Murphy decomposition of settled forecasts. Second, all three fix the hidden state exogenously (an assigned role, a dealt hand) or make it fully endogenous (a relocated regent); none scores forecasts about exogenous future events emerging from agent interaction over multiple horizons, with paired placebo-controlled causal arms. Our testbed combines settlement with calibration (hierarchical Platt per source/confidence band), K-trial aggregation with data-dependent shrinkage, and pre-registered, placebo-controlled causal evaluation in an LLM-driven sandbox.
