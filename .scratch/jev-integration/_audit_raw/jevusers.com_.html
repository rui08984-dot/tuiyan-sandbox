Getting access to Jev

Three routes, as of 2026-09-21. Verify before you build on any of them.

Official TypeSafe waitlist → Official access. Builders report invites arriving the same day, though that is anecdotal and not a commitment. No waitlist OpenRouter → Listed as typesafe/jev-1.13, 32K context, $0.042 per million input tokens and $0 output. Not yet in OpenRouter's public model list, so treat availability as unconfirmed. No waitlist Vercel AI Gateway → Reachable with a Vercel AI Gateway key, no separate signup. Community reports a free window; check current pricing before you depend on it.
